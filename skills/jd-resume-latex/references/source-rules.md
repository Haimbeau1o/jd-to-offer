# Source Rules

## Evidence Priority

1. user-confirmed facts
2. local repo artifacts and prior validated notes
3. official company or product materials
4. official documentation
5. primary papers
6. clearly marked inference

## Claim Levels

### confirmed

Use when a statement is directly supported by known work, artifact, or source.

### defensible

Use when the wording is slightly stronger than raw notes but still consistent with the evidence and likely follow-up questions.

### forbidden

Do not use when the statement would imply:
- ownership the candidate did not have
- business domain experience the candidate did not actually gain
- invented metrics
- methods that were never used
- product impact that cannot be defended

## Packaging Rule

When domain experience is missing, do not fake the domain. Instead:
- prove transferable technical capability
- explicitly bridge to the new domain
- let a project or knowledge pack carry the missing domain proof
